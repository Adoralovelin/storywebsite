Chapter 5

New Look, New Adventures

A fresh seaweed shop and playful secrets

Floofy walked into the newly-furnished seaweed store, gaping at all the new sights—the freshly painted, coral-coloured walls, the fluffy carpets beneath her feet, and the different types of seaweed swaying under the piercing light of the sun.

She snapped out of her trance when Popcorn yelled into her ear, "FLOOFY! I’ve been calling you for the last three minutes!" After hearing this bombastic blast, Floofy felt her face heating up so quickly, she changed the subject.

"Ooh, Seal, when did you add all of these new decorations?" she asked in a hurry. "It’s been only around five days since you got back! How did you do this so efficiently?" After all, Floofy was fairly impressed, Seal and Silver usually being the ones late to everything.

"Oh, it was nothing, really. We just…" Seal trailed off, glancing at Silver, a mischievous glint in her eyes. "I don’t think you want to know…"
